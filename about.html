About me

I’ve had a lifelong interest in art, shaped by a range of influences and a curiosity to keep exploring.

This curiosity sits at the heart of my experimentation across different media and styles, exploring subjects including landscapes, botanical forms, and wildlife. Each piece is an opportunity to try something new and see where it leads me.
