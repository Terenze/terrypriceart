Returns policy

I want you to be happy with your artwork. If you change your mind, please contact me within 14 days of receiving your order.

After you have told me you would like to return an item, you then have a further 14 days to send it back.

How to return an item

Please email me before returning your order so I can confirm the return details.

Returned artwork should be unused, undamaged and packaged securely, preferably in the original packaging where possible.

I recommend using a tracked or recorded delivery service, as I cannot be responsible for returned items that are lost or damaged in transit.

Unless the item is damaged, incorrect or not as described, you are responsible for the cost of returning it.

Refunds

I will process your refund within 14 days of receiving the returned artwork.

Refunds will be made to the original payment method. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to credit the refund to your account.

Where applicable, I will refund the standard delivery cost. If you chose a more expensive delivery option, only the standard delivery amount will be refunded.

Damaged, incorrect or not as described

If your order arrives damaged, is not the item you ordered, or is not as described, please contact me as soon as possible.

Please include photographs of the artwork and packaging so I can understand what has happened and agree the next step with you.

Where the issue is with the artwork or delivery, I will arrange an appropriate refund or replacement where possible, including reasonable return postage costs.

Commissions and custom artwork

Commissioned or custom-made artwork cannot usually be returned unless it arrives damaged, is incorrect, or is not as described.

International orders

For international orders, return postage, customs charges, taxes or import duties are the responsibility of the customer unless the item is damaged, incorrect or not as described.

Contact

If you have any questions, email me at terry.price16@gmail.com.
