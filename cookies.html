Cookie Policy

This website uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies to ensure it works properly and, where you choose to allow it, to understand how it is used.

Analytics (optional)

If you choose to allow analytics cookies, they help us understand how visitors use the site — for example which pages are viewed most often. This information is used only to improve the website.

Analytics cookies are only set if you give your consent.

Marketing (optional)

Marketing cookies would be used to measure the effectiveness of advertising or social media activity. This website does not currently use marketing cookies.

Managing your preferences

When you first visit this website, you can choose whether to accept or reject optional cookies. Your preference is stored on your device.

You can also clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.
